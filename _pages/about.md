I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in Transportation Engineering at the College of Transportation, Tongji University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **computer vision and autonomous driving**, with a particular focus on **3D vision, neural simulation and end-to-end autonomous driving**.

My research aims to build realistic and interactive virtual environments for autonomous driving, and to leverage them for systematic evaluation, diagnosis, and improvement of autonomous driving systems.

# 🚧 Ongoing Research

### Sim-to-Real Evaluation for Autonomous Driving Simulation

Developing systematic evaluation methodologies for measuring how faithfully virtual autonomous driving environments reproduce real-world system behavior, with a focus on both open-loop and closed-loop sim-to-real consistency.


### Diagnosis of End-to-End Autonomous Driving Systems

Investigating systematic approaches for identifying failure-critical temporal windows, traffic participants, and system capabilities in end-to-end autonomous driving, with the goal of quantitatively characterizing model limitations and safety boundaries.


### Hazardous Traffic Scenario Understanding

Studying vision-based and 3D spatial reasoning methods for recognizing and understanding safety-critical traffic interactions, particularly hazardous scenarios involving vulnerable road users and complex vehicle–road-user interactions.
